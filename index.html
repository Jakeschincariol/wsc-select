WSC Select

Additional training, competition, exposure and access, scheduled around your club, never against it.

Four things, added on top.

Each one sits alongside a player’s existing club, never in place of it. Open any of them for the detail.

  • Training

    Curated sessions with elite coaches, built around the individual player rather than a team sheet.

  • Competition

    Entry into some of the most competitive and desirable youth tournaments in the country.

  • Exposure

    A professional environment, in front of the people who decide what happens next.

  • Access

    Standards, facilities and resources most players never experience at this age.

Five moves, in order.

  1. 01

    Find ambitious players

    Identified on ability and attitude, not on who can pay.

  2. 02

    Put them together

    One group, drawn from clubs that never train together.

  3. 03

    Give them elite resources

    Coaching, environments and preparation at a higher standard.

  4. 04

    Create competition

    Real games against the strongest opposition we can find.

  5. 05

    Raise their level

    Everything gained goes back to the player, and to their club.

The focus is the individual player.

Six things are sharpened, and every one of them travels back to their club with them.

  • Technical ability

    The work on the ball that holds up under pressure.

  • Decision making

    Seeing the picture earlier, and choosing better inside it.

  • Confidence

    Belief that survives a bad touch, a bad half, a bad night.

  • Competitiveness

    The habit of treating every session like it counts.

  • Physical preparation

    Moving, recovering and preparing like a serious athlete.

  • Understanding of the game

    Knowing why the game is asking for it, not just what.

Put a player forward.

Selection is by nomination and invitation. Coaches, parents and club staff can all put a player forward.

No fee. No obligation. Nominating does not affect a player’s standing at their current club.
